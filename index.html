    Meal photo — visual log only

    A photo never estimates calories, protein, carbs or fat. Enter those numbers yourself above. The photo is just a reminder of what you ate.

    Hand-based portion guide

    • Palm = about 20–30 g protein (chicken, fish, tofu)
    • Fist = about 1 cup carbs (rice, pasta, oats)
    • Cupped hand = about ½ cup carbs / fruit
    • Thumb = about 1 tablespoon fat (oil, butter, peanut butter)

    Watch & import

    Amazfit / Zepp (and Apple Health)

    There is no live watch hookup in this app — a web page cannot connect directly to Zepp. The honest route is:

    1. In the Zepp app on your phone, turn on sync to Apple Health (Zepp → Profile → Apple Health, or Health app → Sharing).
    2. Each morning, copy the numbers from Zepp into the Today form, or paste the shared stats text in the Autofill hub.
    3. Or export a CSV from Zepp / Apple Health and import it below — it fills past days in bulk.

    Import CSV

    CSV with a header row, e.g. date,sleep_hours,sleep_score,deep_min,rem_min,resting_hr,hrv_ms,stress,steps,weight_kg. Existing days are not duplicated — re-importing the same date replaces it.

    Settings — your Groq API key (optional)

    AI screenshot reading is optional and uses your own Groq API key. Get a free one at console.groq.com — sign up with email, no credit card — then paste it here. Your key is stored only in this browser's localStorage. It is never sent to any server except Groq, never put in the code, and never uploaded to GitHub. Only the status “key saved” is shown here — the key itself is never displayed.

    Without a key, screenshot reading stays reference-only, and paste-as-text, CSV import and manual entry still work.
